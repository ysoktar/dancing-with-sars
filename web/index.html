Planetary surface fingerprint explorer. The globe is covered with real radar images from the NASA–ISRO NISAR satellite, one 12-day orbit cycle at a time. L-band radar records how rough and wet the ground is (HH) and how much plant volume stands on it (HV). Draw any area to read its fingerprint against a Brazilian radar library, map its crops and soil moisture, or look for landslides and spills.
